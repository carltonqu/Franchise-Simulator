Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

How accurate are the simulations?

Our AI models are trained on thousands of real franchise data points. While no prediction is 100% accurate, our simulations provide reliable estimates based on your specific inputs and market conditions.

Can I compare multiple opportunities?

Yes! Our Professional and Enterprise plans allow you to save and compare unlimited scenarios side by side, making it easy to evaluate different brands, locations, or investment levels.

What data do I need to provide?

Basic inputs include franchise type, initial investment, location, monthly rent, labor costs, and expected sales. The more details you provide, the more accurate your simulation will be.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and never share your data with third parties. Your simulation data is stored securely and you can delete it at any time.
